Date & Time Functions
----------------------
* CURDATE()--> CURRENT DATE.
* CURTIME()--> CURRENT TIME.
* NOW() --> CURRENT DATE AND TIME.
* DATE() --> EXTRACTED DATE PART
* TIME() --> EXTRACTED TIME PART.
* YEAR() --> EXTRACT YEAR
* MONTH() --> EXTRACT MONTH
* DAY()/DAYOFMONTH() : EXTRACT DAY OF THE MONTH.
* WEEK() --> EXTRACT WEEK NUMBER.
* DAYOFWEEK() --> EXTRACT DAY OF THE WEEK.
* DATE_ADD() --> ADD TIME TO A DATE
* DATE_SUB() --> SUBTRACT TIME FROM THE TABLE.
* DATEDIFF() --> DIFFERENCE BETWEEN TWO DATES.
* TIMESTAMPDIFF() --> DIFFERENCE BETWEEN TWO DATETIME VALUES.

CREATE TABLE employee_attendance
(
	emp_id INT PRIMARY KEY AUTO_INCREMENT,
	emp_name VARCHAR(100),
	department VARCHAR(50),
	joining_date DATE,
	birth_date DATE,
	login_time DATETIME,
	logout_time DATETIME,
	salary DECIMAL(10,2)
);
